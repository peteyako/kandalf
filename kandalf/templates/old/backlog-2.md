# Backlog

Work not yet scheduled, sorted by kind. Tiles follow the rules in the header of [plans.md](plans.md) and keep their `{key}-NN` key wherever they go. Drag one onto the Promote column and it moves to the top of To do on the plans board. A tile in the Design decision column is answered on its card, not promoted; a story that waits on it says `Blocked by` and its key.
